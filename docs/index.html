Scan a document

This page asks the highbridge app on your computer to scan with your TWAIN scanner and sends the pages back here. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How this works
  1. The page talks to highbridge at http://127.0.0.1:47116. Your browser may ask to let this site reach apps on your device. Allow it.
  2. The first time, highbridge asks you to approve this website. After that, Scan starts the scanner straight away.
  3. If highbridge is not running, the page opens a highbridge:// link to start it. If it is not installed, the page links to the one-time installer.

Documents

Scanned documents will appear here. Each file stacks that scan's pages from top to bottom.

    Read a card

    This page asks the highbridge app on your computer to read a payment card. For the application it selects, the result includes the full card number, the expiry, and the cardholder name. highbridge runs on this computer and gives that number only to this page.

    How this works
    1. The page talks to highbridge at http://127.0.0.1:47116. Your browser may ask to let this site reach apps on your device. Allow it.
    2. The first time, highbridge asks you to approve this website. The prompt says the full card number, the expiry, and the cardholder name are sent to this page over the local connection. After that, Read card starts straight away.
    3. The page only sends the reader, the interface, an optional application id, and how long to wait. highbridge chooses every card command. The card number comes from the chip. Tracks and raw card data are not sent back.
